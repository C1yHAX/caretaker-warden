## Changelog 1.0.4
EN: World: seven weather presets for the current session and a switch back to natural weather.
EN: World: "Repair everything nearby" within 10, 25, 50, 100 or 250 m, with progress and cancel; repairs also refresh the damage visuals of supported objects.
EN: Boat repair targets the actual damaged part; only modules of your own boat are included.
EN: Boat engines: cylinders are fitted with the game's own method (Basic, Booster, Super Booster and Cheat Booster for diesel and electric engines).
EN: Items: the catalog and all spawning skip build-only objects such as large tanks, batteries and generators; new Lazarus package with human seeds, food, nutrients and memory objects.
EN: Fixes a crash during area repair caused by access to an expired object.
